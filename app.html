Privacy & Safety

LaffPak is a reaction meme database and organization platform designed for discovering, saving, organizing, and sharing reaction images and internet humor content through a mobile-first social experience.

LaffPak was created and continues to be monitored and operated 100% by LUNUSArc.

Our goal is to provide a fast, creative, and community-driven platform while maintaining user safety, platform integrity, and responsible moderation practices.

WARNING : Excessive social media use can result in mental, physical or otherwise spiritual stress - please use with caution. Installing LaffPak may result in excessive laughter, loss of productivity, and/or addiction to memes.

User Content

Users may upload images, tags, comments, profile information, and other content to LaffPak.

By using the platform, users agree not to upload:

  • Illegal material
  • Exploitative or abusive content
  • Terrorist or extremist propaganda
  • Harassment or targeted abuse
  • Copyright violations
  • Malicious spam or deceptive activity

LaffPak reserves the right to remove content, restrict accounts, or moderate platform activity at any time.

NSFW Content

LaffPak includes optional NSFW visibility settings.

Users must confirm they are 18 years of age or older before enabling NSFW visibility.

Illegal exploitative content is strictly prohibited.

Account & Data

LaffPak may collect limited account and platform information including:

  • Email addresses
  • Usernames
  • Uploaded content
  • Profile information
  • Usage activity related to platform functionality

Authentication and backend services may be provided through third-party providers including Discord and Supabase infrastructure services.

LaffPak does not currently sell personal user data.

Moderation & Safety

LaffPak uses moderation systems including reporting tools, anti-spam protections, upload restrictions, and visibility filtering.

Reported content may be reviewed and removed if found to violate platform guidelines.

Availability

LaffPak is an evolving platform currently under active development. Features, systems, moderation tools, and policies may change over time.

Use of LaffPak is provided “as is” without guarantees of uninterrupted availability or permanent data retention.
